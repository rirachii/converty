FFmpeg
This local build bundles an existing self-contained FFmpeg 8.0 executable.
FFmpeg copyright (c) 2000-2025 the FFmpeg developers.
The executable is GPL-enabled and includes third-party codec libraries.
Its exact version, configuration, and source executable hash are in FFmpeg-build.txt.
Upstream: https://ffmpeg.org/
Source release: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.0.tar.xz

This is a local development build, not a prepared public binary release.
Before redistributing binaries, provide corresponding source and build material
for the exact FFmpeg build and all its linked libraries, retain their notices,
and perform the signing and notarization release checks in macOS/README.md.
The build script does not download an unverifiable binary automatically.

libarchive
Converty uses macOS's system libarchive. C declarations are vendored from
libarchive v3.7.7, under the BSD terms retained verbatim in both header files:
https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive/tree/v3.7.7/libarchive
The public headers are not a bundled copy of the libarchive implementation.

Apple frameworks and SF Symbols are system-provided. Converty does not include
Tangerine code, branding, artwork, or demo footage.
